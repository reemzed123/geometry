## Features

- **Geometry construction** — create and edit shapes, points, and lines.
- **Angles and measurements** — label and explore angles and geometric relationships.
- **Function graphing** — plot and investigate mathematical functions.
- **3D solids** — visualize and work with three-dimensional shapes.
- **Freehand drawing** — sketch directly on the board.
- **Save and export** — keep and share your work in supported formats.
